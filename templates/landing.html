FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about how NEXT LEAP 2.0 guides your development.

What is the "Your Next LEAP" feature?
"Your Next LEAP" is our signature prioritization engine. It analyzes your target career, current skill gaps, pending roadmap milestones, and active projects to deliver one concrete, high-leverage learning task. When you complete it, the engine recalculates your next best move.
Do I need prior technical background to use NEXT LEAP?
Not at all. When you register, you can indicate your background and existing competencies. The roadmap generator will calibrate Phase 1 to establish core foundations before moving into complex engineering or analytics.
How does the AI Career Coach work?
The AI Career Coach provides real-time guidance on career options, skill improvement, project ideation, and interview readiness. It can run via configured LLM APIs (Gemini or OpenAI) or via our built-in deterministic expert system when an external API is not configured.
Can I change my target career goal later?
Yes! You can explore different careers in the "Discover Careers" module at any time and set a new target. When you do, NEXT LEAP automatically recalibrates your skill gap analysis and generates an updated roadmap.
